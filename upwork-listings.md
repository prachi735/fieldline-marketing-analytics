## Automated Marketing API Pipeline in Python and SQL

**Role** · Data engineer and marketing analytics developer

**Description** · I built a synthetic marketing API and an incremental reporting pipeline for five advertising accounts. The pipeline paginates through campaign data, retries throttled requests and commits corrections with a watermark. A replay test loaded 240 campaign-day records, updated five existing rows and processed zero new rows on the next run. The sample includes SQLite and PostgreSQL results, execution logs and an offline report.

**Skills to select where available** · Python, SQL, API Integration, PostgreSQL, Data Engineering

**Demo** · Show the initial load, the five late corrections and the replay. Attach the captioned walkthrough or share the channel totals.

## B2B Marketing ROI and Multi-Touch Attribution

**Role** · Marketing analytics consultant

**Description** · In this synthetic B2B case study, I traced 3,200 lead journeys from paid touches to booked revenue. I compared first-touch, last-touch and linear attribution in SQL. Review Sites received $294K under first touch and $1.17M under last touch. I reconciled every model to the same 171 wins, compared media CAC and gross-profit ROI, and modeled the downside and upside of a $10K budget test.

**Skills** · Marketing Analytics, Attribution Modeling, SQL, Data Visualization, Python

**Demo** · Switch credit models, follow the Review Sites row and open the budget sensitivity table.

## E-commerce Customer Retention, RFM and Lifetime Value

**Role** · Customer analytics consultant

**Description** · I analyzed 3,434 synthetic orders from 1,500 buyers to identify when customers return and which segments merit a retention test. I built a monthly cohort heatmap, scored each customer by recency, frequency and spend, and estimated contribution through the next quarter. The forecast ran 1.95% above the held-out quarter’s aggregate outcome. I identified 319 at-risk repeat buyers for a controlled win-back offer.

**Skills** · Customer Segmentation, Cohort Analysis, Predictive Analytics, SQL, Python

**Demo** · Show the heatmap, the at-risk segment and the holdout results together.

## Marketing Data Quality and Spend Reconciliation Audit

**Role** · Marketing data quality analyst

**Description** · I audited a synthetic multi-currency campaign extract against its source ledger. Ten SQL rules generated 13 findings covering duplicate and missing records, an unmapped currency, taxonomy drift, delayed loads and KPI discrepancies. I built a daily spend reconciliation and an exception queue with the affected record, evidence, owner and proposed correction. The audit also identified an unpriced row requiring resolution before final USD sign-off.

**Skills** · Data Quality, Data Validation, SQL, Data Analysis, Data Cleaning

**Demo** · Show the daily variance and filter the exception queue to high severity. Open the scorecard to identify the owner.
