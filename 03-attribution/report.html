A $10,000 reallocation test

Move spend from Display to Review Sites. Assume the removed dollars lose Display’s average linear-attributed yield. Vary the new dollars’ yield as a fraction of the Review Sites average. Total spend stays fixed.

ScenarioDestination yieldRevenue changeGross profit change

Decision

Review Sites receives $294,000 under first touch and $1,171,000 under last touch. Test a capped reallocation with a holdout before committing the full budget.
Assumptions and scope

Independent B2B sample with one closed acquisition cohort and four paid channels. Every lead has an ordered path of one to four paid touches. Linear credit is divided equally across touch events. Each win represents one new customer. Gross-profit ROI equals (credited revenue × 72% gross margin − media spend) / media spend. Model differences measure attribution sensitivity. Incremental impact requires an experiment.
