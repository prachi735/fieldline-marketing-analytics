Decision

Assumptions and scope

Independent March 2025 fixture. Platform records are the reference at date × account × campaign grain. Native amounts are integer minor units. Fixed illustrative USD rates are 1.08 per EUR and 1.27 per GBP. Each row is converted and rounded to cents. Unmapped currencies are excluded from convertible spend and counted separately. A key may fail multiple rules.
