Measurement methodology and data dictionary

All data is synthetic and generated deterministically at the campaign-month grain. Five clients each have two campaigns per channel. Spend uses fixed annual client allocations and monthly weights. Each campaign-month tracks spend, a planned qualified-lead target, leads, qualified leads, opportunities, created pipeline, wins and booked revenue. CSV columns use USD for monetary amounts.

A qualified lead is a deduplicated lead who meets the client's fit and intent criteria. Each lead and its downstream outcomes are assigned to one campaign using a simulated first paid touch. Results use the month of that touch and assume fully matured cohorts. Created pipeline sums opportunity value at creation and includes won opportunities. Booked revenue sums closed-won deal value. Attribution is descriptive and does not establish incrementality.

Cost per qualified lead divides spend by qualified leads. Media CAC divides ad spend by won deals and excludes sales and overhead. Pipeline multiple divides created pipeline by spend. Ratios use aggregated totals within the selected filters. A dash indicates a zero denominator.

The review rule flags campaigns with at least $5,000 in selected spend and a pipeline multiple below 1×. Flagged spend is exposure to review. Recommended actions are hypotheses to test. The 3× pipeline threshold used for scaling candidates is illustrative.
