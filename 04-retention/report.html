Forecast check

For 1,102 existing buyers, the unadjusted Q4 estimate was $61,857. Applying the 68.4% historical carryover produced $42,332 against $41,523 observed. Average absolute error was $43 per buyer, against mean realized contribution of $38. Use this forecast for aggregate budgeting. Individual offer targeting needs separate validation.

Decision

Test a win-back offer with a holdout among 319 at-risk repeat buyers. Use segment-level contribution to set the incentive budget.
Assumptions and scope

Independent e-commerce sample covering January–December 2025. Month 0 is the first purchase. Retention counts a customer again in every month with an order. RFM means recency, frequency and monetary value. Scores use fixed thresholds in the Python source. Value uses a 55% contribution margin. The estimate combines observed contribution with a three-month forecast and excludes subsequent value. Q2-to-Q3 carryover is trained on customers acquired before Q3, then frozen for the Q4 holdout. The forecast uses prior-quarter contribution for customers present at the forecast origin.
